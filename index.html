Our story

Tea, but make it social.

Blossom & Barley started with a simple idea: tea doesn't have to be quiet. We swapped the teapot for a big jug, added lots of ice and called our friends.

Our name says it all. Blossom stands for light, floral flavours. Barley stands for the good things you share: a picnic, a long table, a sunny afternoon.

Every blend is made to be brewed in big batches, poured over ice and passed around.

Pretty enough to post, tasty enough to share.

How to brew

Easy as 1, 2, tea.

  1. STEP 1

    Steep

    2 bags in 250 ml hot water for 5 minutes.

  2. STEP 2

    Chill

    Let it cool and pour over lots of ice.

  3. STEP 3

    Share

    With your crew. Repeat.
